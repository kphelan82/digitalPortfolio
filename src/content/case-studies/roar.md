    paragraphs:
      - >-
        This was a project based on a hypothetical scenario specifically done for a certification I earned, so I had full control of the UX/UI, animation and visual design from start to finish.
  - title: "The problem"
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        Roar Cycles is a small company in need of a new website that would reach a wider audience of young adult riders with an eye for quality craftsmanship. They have a few stores and also wanted to streamline their online sales process. Each bike is built custom based on 3 model types and they also have apparel and merchandise for purchase. 
  - title: "The solution"
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        A custom site and mobile app that showcases the 3 bike models and displays various customization options, an online merch shop, and store finder. The primary goal was allowing customers to place and track online orders.

  - title: "Lessons learned"
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        At this point, I had done a UX certification or two online and had plenty of real agency experience, but I had just gotten laid off from my job after over 10 years. I decided to focus on this as a side project while I was job hunting and it was really the first time I could use my entire hybrid experience design/visual design skill set in a complete way. Looking back, there are plenty of things I'd change knowing what I know now and given the tools I live in day to day. I think it highlights how quickly things have changed in the industry in just a few years, but proof that I never really stop learning.
