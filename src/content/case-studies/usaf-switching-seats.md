sections:
  - title: "The problem"
    paragraphs:
      - "The team was asked to develop an interactive micro-site to support a large U.S. Air Force recruitment initiative that coincided with their sponsorship of NASCAR driver Reed Sorenson."
  - title: "The solution"
    paragraphs:
      - "A full 360-degree panoramic experience that highlighted how skills gained in Air Force training could be applied to a variety of situations, as a NASCAR pit crew and driver switched places with a team of USAF Airmen. The site featured sortable video content, full crew bios, user Q&A and polls, custom map integration, downloadable resources, and access to Air Force career advisors."
      - "As Interactive Art Director for the project, I handled visual and interaction design for the individual panels and content screens. Working alongside a Lead Programmer in a direct partnership, I established interaction patterns and animation styles, my partner identified technical feasibilities, and when problems arose, we tested solutions in real time. The end result was 100% custom UI and a robust 3D experience, built in a very tight time window."
  - title: "The impact"
    paragraphs:
      - "This site was only live for the duration of the Air Force’s NASCAR campaign, which lasted one season. During that time, it won a Design Licks Site of the Day award and a Dope Award. In addition, it generated over 4,000 qualified recruitment leads, which was a 175% increase over the previous sponsorship. The site had 106 MM impressions, and users averaged over 5 mins. on the site per visit. To this day, it is one of the most unique sites that ever went live."
