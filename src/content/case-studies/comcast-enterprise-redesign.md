  - title: "The new front door"
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        We launched a new front door to serve every segment with personalized pathways that help people find what they're looking for faster. The old homepage was focused on small business ecommerce. Now visitors self-identify as small business, enterprise or public sector from the first screen.
  - title: "Gated content and lead generation"
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        Gated content that speaks to enterprise interests is the backbone of the lead generation strategy, and our 2024 Cybersecurity Awareness Month campaign is a good example.
      - >-
        We turned the cybersecurity solutions page into a content hub and put our Cybersecurity Threat Report, built on proprietary research, behind a gated lead form as the hero asset. We placed it strategically across the site and pointed every marketing channel at the page. Lead generation increased 5x.
  - title: "The enterprise homepage"
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        The enterprise homepage is the linchpin of the strategy. We added a customer logo carousel, technology trends, solutions, and case studies with quotes from leading brands so customers can share their experience. Updated language and branding support our SEO efforts, and bucketing solutions lets visitors scan the product range quickly.
  - title: "The public sector hub"
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        Public sector is another growth priority. User research showed these organizations often don't think of themselves as enterprises: they buy through RFPs and contract negotiations rather than a buy flow or a sales consultation, so we took a different approach.
      - >-
        We redesigned the section around three pathways (federal government, state and local government, and education). Each one has content tailored to that audience's interests and purchasing needs, with public service and digital equity front and center.
  - title: "Industry and solution pages"
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        Retail, financial services, hospitality and healthcare are some of our key verticals, so we rebranded those industry pages with content that's more relevant to each audience and shows our expertise in those spaces. We also updated the SD-WAN, secure networking and dedicated internet pages to showcase our evolved solutions.
